Bitbucket Contribution Chart

Connect your Bitbucket Cloud account to show a GitHub-style contribution heatmap on your profile.

Create an API token at Atlassian API tokens with scopes read:user:bitbucket, read:workspace:bitbucket, and read:repository:bitbucket.

Credentials are stored in chrome.storage.sync on your device only. On Bitbucket, click the extension toolbar icon to open the chart.
